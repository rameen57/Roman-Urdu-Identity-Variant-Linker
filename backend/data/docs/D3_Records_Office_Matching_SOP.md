# Records Office Matching SOP

This SOP defines how the Records Office decides whether two records refer to the same person. The decision is never made by the agent alone; the agent presents evidence and asks the officer to confirm.

Matching bands:
1. Likely same: combined score >= 0.92 AND all name tokens are phonetically equal AND at least one supporting field agrees (father name phonetic match, birth year within 1 year, or city resolves to the same place).
2. Possible: combined score between 0.75 and 0.92, OR name tokens differ phonetically. Explain uncertainty and ask for more fields.
3. Unlikely: combined score below 0.75. State it is unlikely and why.
4. Hard conflict: birth years differ by more than 1 year. Reject and explain.

All data and rules in this SOP are fictional demonstration data.
