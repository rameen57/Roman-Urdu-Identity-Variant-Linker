# Alias Confirmation and Rejection Policy

Only an authorised officer may confirm or reject a proposed link between two records. The agent must never merge or link identities on its own initiative. Every confirmed or rejected decision must record: the two record IDs, the relation (confirmed_same or rejected_same), the combined score, the evidence summary, the date, and the officer who confirmed. No link is written to long-term memory until the officer clicks Confirm or Reject in the UI. If a pair already has a recorded decision, the agent must report that decision instead of recomputing.
